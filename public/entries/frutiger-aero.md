# Frutiger Aero

A retrospective label for glossy, optimistic interfaces and imagery that combine nature with consumer technology.

Platform: style
Terminology: Retrospective label
Aliases: aero aesthetic, glossy nature tech

## Descriptions people use

- glossy grass bubbles old windows future
- blue sky green grass glass buttons

## Defining signals

- **Nature and technology** (Visual signal): A green landscape appears inside a technological surface.
- **Glossy aqua surfaces** (Visual signal): The button uses an aqua-like shine and highlight.
- **Sky blue and grass green** (Visual signal): Blue sky and green landscape establish the natural palette.
- **Reflections and bubbles** (Visual signal): Translucent bubbles supply a glossy, reflective motif.

## Behavior

Combine recognizable natural cues with glossy technological surfaces. Give content a stable reading area and avoid treating every blue gradient as sufficient evidence of this style.
