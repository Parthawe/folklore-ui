# Skeuomorphism

Digital controls borrow the materials, forms, or operating metaphors of physical objects.

Platform: style
Terminology: Established term
Aliases: real-world UI, material imitation

## Descriptions people use

- app looks like leather notebook
- buttons look like real hardware

## Defining signals

- **Physical metaphor** (Visual signal): The notebook supplies a familiar physical reference.
- **Material cues** (Visual signal): The binding and paper-like surface suggest construction.
- **Consistent light and depth** (Visual signal): A consistent light direction gives the button apparent depth.

## Behavior

Choose one useful physical metaphor. Keep its lighting and controls coherent while preserving text clarity and familiar keyboard behavior.

## Build prompt

Create a skeuomorphism visual direction for the current project. Choose one useful physical metaphor. Keep its lighting and controls coherent while preserving text clarity and familiar keyboard behavior. Preserve the existing task flow, content, accessible contrast, clear focus, and reduced-motion support.

## Debug prompt

Check decorative texture hiding labels, inconsistent lighting, and a physical metaphor that makes the task harder to understand.
