# Neobrutalism

A graphic web style using bold outlines, flat color, blunt type, and hard offset shadows.

Platform: style
Terminology: Industry-coined trend
Aliases: neo-brutalism, neo brutalist

## Descriptions people use

- bright blocks black outlines hard shadows
- yellow cards thick black border

## Defining signals

- **Heavy outlines** (Visual signal): Thick, consistent outlines define the control boundaries.
- **Flat saturated color** (Visual signal): Color appears in solid blocks rather than realistic shading.
- **Hard offset shadow** (Visual signal): The control casts a crisp displaced shadow.
- **Blunt typography** (Visual signal): Heavy, direct lettering gives the composition its voice.

## Behavior

Use a small palette of solid colors, consistent thick outlines, and hard shadows. Keep form controls and focus clearly distinguishable from graphic decoration.
