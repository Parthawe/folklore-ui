# Skeuomorphism

Digital controls borrow the materials, forms, or operating metaphors of physical objects.

Platform: style
Terminology: Established term
Aliases: real-world UI, material imitation

## Descriptions people use

- app looks like leather notebook
- buttons look like real hardware

## Defining signals

- **Physical metaphor** (Visual signal): Signal 1 in this specimen.
- **Material cues** (Visual signal): Signal 2 in this specimen.
- **Consistent light and depth** (Visual signal): Signal 3 in this specimen.

Choose one useful physical metaphor. Keep its lighting and controls coherent while preserving text clarity and familiar keyboard behavior.
